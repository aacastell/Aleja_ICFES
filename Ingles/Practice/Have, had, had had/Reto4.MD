# 📘 TEXTO

Last year, Daniel applied for a job at an international company. By the time he received a response, he already (1) \_\_\_\_\_\_ another offer. However, he still attended the interview because he (2) \_\_\_\_\_\_ about the company for a long time.

During the interview, the manager asked him about his experience. Daniel explained that he (3) \_\_\_\_\_\_ in similar roles before, which made him feel confident. The manager seemed impressed because Daniel (4) \_\_\_\_\_\_ several complex projects in the past.

After the interview, Daniel felt satisfied. He realized that he (5) \_\_\_\_\_\_ a very strong impression. A few days later, the company contacted him again. They told him that they (6) \_\_\_\_\_\_ all the candidates and decided he was the best.

In the end, Daniel accepted the new position because he (7) \_\_\_\_\_\_ a better opportunity.

# ✅ RESPUESTAS + EXPLICACIÓN (CLAVE PARA ENSEÑAR)

## 1. ✅ B) had accepted

👉 “By the time…” → acción anterior a otra pasada  
→ ya había aceptado otra oferta

***

## 2. ✅ B) had heard

👉 querer mostrar conocimiento previo antes de la entrevista  
→ pasado anterior

***

## 3. ✅ C) had worked

👉 experiencia anterior al momento de la entrevista

***

## 4. ✅ C) had completed

👉 proyectos completados antes del momento del discurso

***

## 5. ✅ B) had made

👉 reflexión después → la impresión ocurrió antes

***

## 6. ✅ C) evaluated

👉 NO necesitas past perfect aquí  
👉 es una secuencia narrativa normal

🔥 Esto es TRAMPA TÍPICA ICFES

***

## 7. ✅ C) had found

👉 encontró una mejor oportunidad antes de decidir

***

# 🎯 ANÁLISIS GLOBAL (esto es lo que debes enseñar)

## 🧠 Idea clave del texto:

Es una historia en pasado → pero con **eventos anteriores al pasado principal**

***

## 🔥 Patrones que el estudiante debe detectar:

### 1. “By the time…”

→ ✅ past perfect obligatorio

***

### 2. Explicaciones de experiencia previa

→ ✅ past perfect ("had worked", "had completed")

***

### 3. Narración principal

→ ✅ simple past ("evaluated")

***

### 4. Reflexión posterior

→ ✅ past perfect (acción ocurrió antes de reflexionar)

***

# 🧠 Regla de oro

> “El tiempo verbal depende de la posición del evento en la historia, no de la oración individual.”

***
