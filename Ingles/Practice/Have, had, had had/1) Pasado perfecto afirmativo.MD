Perfecto—ahora hacemos lo mismo pero con:

* ✅ **Past Perfect: had + past participle**
* ✅ **“had had”** (caso especial)

Mantengo variedad en verbos y complementos.

***

# ✅ 1. I

### Ejemplo (Past Perfect):

* I **had finished** my homework before dinner.

### Preguntas:

1. I \_\_\_\_\_\_ completed the report before the meeting started. (have / had)
2. I had \_\_\_\_\_\_ a mistake in the document. (make / made)
3. I had \_\_\_\_\_\_ the email before my boss called me. (send / sent)
4. I \_\_\_\_\_\_ learned that lesson before the exam. (have / had)
5. I had \_\_\_\_\_\_ all the instructions carefully. (follow / followed)

***

# ✅ 2. He / She

### Ejemplo:

* She **had finished** her homework before going out.

### Preguntas:

1. He \_\_\_\_\_\_ forgotten his password before the test. (have / had)
2. She had \_\_\_\_\_\_ a reservation at the restaurant. (make / made)
3. He had \_\_\_\_\_\_ the problem without help. (solve / solved)
4. She \_\_\_\_\_\_ prepared everything in advance. (have / had)
5. He had \_\_\_\_\_\_ a similar situation in the past. (experience / experienced)

***

# ✅ 3. It

### Ejemplo:

* It **had stopped** raining before we left.

### Preguntas:

1. It \_\_\_\_\_\_ caused a delay in the system. (have / had)
2. It had \_\_\_\_\_\_ a serious issue before the update. (be / been)
3. It had \_\_\_\_\_\_ faster than expected last year. (grow / grown)
4. It \_\_\_\_\_\_ created confusion among users. (have / had)
5. It had \_\_\_\_\_\_ an effect on the results. (have / had)

***

# ✅ 4. They

### Ejemplo:

* They **had finished** the project before the deadline.

### Preguntas:

1. They \_\_\_\_\_\_ built the structure before winter arrived. (have / had)
2. They had \_\_\_\_\_\_ a new method for solving the problem. (develop / developed)
3. They had \_\_\_\_\_\_ all the resources available. (use / used)
4. They \_\_\_\_\_\_ discovered the error earlier. (have / had)
5. They had \_\_\_\_\_\_ the instructions incorrectly. (interpret / interpreted)

***

# ✅ 5. We

### Ejemplo:

* We **had finished** our homework before the class started.

### Preguntas:

1. We \_\_\_\_\_\_ planned everything before the trip began. (have / had)
2. We had \_\_\_\_\_\_ a better solution earlier. (find / found)
3. We had \_\_\_\_\_\_ all the data before the analysis. (collect / collected)
4. We \_\_\_\_\_\_ discussed that topic before the meeting. (have / had)
5. We had \_\_\_\_\_\_ the project on time. (complete / completed)

***

# 🔥 Ahora: sección especial “HAD HAD”

Aquí introduces explícitamente el patrón que más cuesta.

***

# ✅ HAD HAD (todos los sujetos)

### Ejemplo:

* I **had had** breakfast before the exam.  
  (= Había tenido / había comido)

***

### Preguntas:

1. I \_\_\_\_\_\_ had enough time to prepare before the interview. (have / had)
2. She had \_\_\_\_\_\_ a similar experience before that job. (have / had)
3. They \_\_\_\_\_\_ had many opportunities in the past. (have / had)
4. We had \_\_\_\_\_\_ several problems before the solution worked. (have / had)
5. He \_\_\_\_\_\_ had a better result in the previous test. (have / had)

***

# 🎯 Clave pedagógica (muy importante)

Haz que el estudiante diga en voz alta:

👉 “had = pasado del pasado”  
👉 “had + participle = acción anterior a otra acción pasada”

Y para **had had**:

👉 “primer had = auxiliar”  
👉 “segundo had = ‘tener’ en participio”
