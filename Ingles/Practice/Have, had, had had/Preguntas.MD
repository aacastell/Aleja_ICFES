Perfecto—ahora pasamos a **preguntas (questions)** usando:

* ✅ **Present Perfect → have/has + participle**
* ✅ **Past Perfect → had + participle**
* ✅ incluyendo **“had had”**

Formato:

* 1 ejemplo por sujeto
* 5 preguntas similares (variadas, sin repetición)

***

# ✅ 1. I

## Ejemplo (Present Perfect):

* **Have I finished** my homework?

### Preguntas:

1. \_\_\_\_\_\_ I visited that museum before? (Have / Has)
2. Have I \_\_\_\_\_\_ the instructions correctly? (follow / followed)
3. \_\_\_\_\_\_ I ever tried sushi? (Have / Had)
4. Have I \_\_\_\_\_\_ any mistakes in this report? (make / made)
5. \_\_\_\_\_\_ I seen this movie already? (Have / Has)

***

## Ejemplo (Past Perfect):

* **Had I finished** my homework before dinner?

### Preguntas:

1. \_\_\_\_\_\_ I completed the task before the deadline? (Had / Have)
2. Had I \_\_\_\_\_\_ the message before you called? (read / readed)
3. \_\_\_\_\_\_ I prepared enough for the exam? (Had / Have)
4. Had I \_\_\_\_\_\_ to that place before last year? (go / gone)
5. \_\_\_\_\_\_ I learned that concept earlier? (Had / Have)

***

# 🎯 Tip didáctico clave

Haz que el estudiante verbalice:

👉 Present perfect → “Have/Has + participio → conexión con el presente”  
👉 Past perfect → “Had + participio → pasado anterior a otro pasado”  
👉 Had had → “había tenido”
