Perfecto—ahora subimos calidad: **cada pregunta con verbo y complemento diferente**, evitando repetición mecánica.

Seguimos con **present perfect (have/has + participle)**.

***

# ✅ 1. I

### Ejemplo:

* I **have finished** my homework.

### Preguntas:

1. I \_\_\_\_\_\_ visited my grandmother in Medellín. (have / has)
2. I have \_\_\_\_\_\_ a new phone this year. (buy / bought)
3. I have \_\_\_\_\_\_ a difficult decision recently. (make / made)
4. I \_\_\_\_\_\_ traveled to another country before. (have / has)
5. I have \_\_\_\_\_\_ an interesting article about AI. (read / reads / reading)

***

# ✅ 2. He / She

### Ejemplo:

* She **has finished** her homework.

### Preguntas:

1. He \_\_\_\_\_\_ lost his keys at the airport. (have / has)
2. She has \_\_\_\_\_\_ a beautiful painting for the exhibition. (create / created)
3. He has \_\_\_\_\_\_ a new job in Bogotá. (find / found)
4. She \_\_\_\_\_\_ learned how to cook Italian food. (have / has)
5. He has \_\_\_\_\_\_ the latest Marvel movie. (watch / watched)

***

# ✅ 3. It

### Ejemplo:

* It **has stopped** raining.

### Preguntas:

1. It \_\_\_\_\_\_ become very expensive lately. (have / has)
2. It has \_\_\_\_\_\_ a big impact on the environment. (have / had)
3. It has \_\_\_\_\_\_ an important role in history. (play / played)
4. It \_\_\_\_\_\_ caused many problems in the city. (have / has)
5. It has \_\_\_\_\_\_ much faster than expected. (grow / grown)

***

# ✅ 4. They

### Ejemplo:

* They **have finished** the project.

### Preguntas:

1. They \_\_\_\_\_\_ built a bridge in that area. (have / has)
2. They have \_\_\_\_\_\_ a lot of money this year. (earn / earned)
3. They have \_\_\_\_\_\_ several countries in Europe. (visit / visited)
4. They \_\_\_\_\_\_ solved the technical issue already. (have / has)
5. They have \_\_\_\_\_\_ a new strategy for the company. (develop / developed)

***

# ✅ 5. We

### Ejemplo:

* We **have finished** our homework.

### Preguntas:

1. We \_\_\_\_\_\_ organized a successful event. (have / has)
2. We have \_\_\_\_\_\_ a complex problem together. (solve / solved)
3. We have \_\_\_\_\_\_ a new approach to the project. (try / tried)
4. We \_\_\_\_\_\_ improved our English skills this year. (have / has)
5. We have \_\_\_\_\_\_ an agreement with the client. (reach / reached)

***

# 🎯 Sugerencia didáctica (clave para ICFES)

**no solo escoja**, sino que explique:

👉 “Uso *have* porque el sujeto es *I / we / they*”  
👉 “Uso *has* porque es *he / she / it*”  
👉 “El segundo verbo debe ir en participio”
