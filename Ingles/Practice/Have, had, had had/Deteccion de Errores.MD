# 📝 MINI EXAMEN ICFES – DETECCIÓN DE ERRORES

## 🔹 Instrucciones

Cada oración tiene un error en el uso de:

* present perfect
* past perfect
* o participio

👉 El estudiante debe:

1. Identificar el error
2. Corregir la oración

***

## 🔹 Sección 1: Oraciones individuales

### 1.

I **has finished** my homework already.

***

### 2.

She **have seen** that movie many times.

***

### 3.

By the time we arrived, the game **has started**.

***

### 4.

They **had went** to the meeting before it was canceled.

***

### 5.

He **have just eaten** lunch.

***

### 6.

When I got home, my brother **has left**.

***

### 7.

We **had completed** the project last week.

***

### 8.

It **have become** very expensive recently.

***

### 9.

I **had saw** that mistake before.

***

### 10.

She **has went** to the store already.

***

# 🔹 Sección 2: Errores con “had had”

### 11.

I **had have** a problem before the exam.

***

### 12.

They **had had had** many opportunities in the past.

***

### 13.

She **has had had** a similar experience before.

***

***

# 🔹 Sección 3: Texto tipo ICFES

### 14–16

> By the time we arrived at the restaurant, it (14) **has closed**.  
> We realized we (15) **have made** a mistake earlier.  
> Fortunately, we (16) **had have** enough money to go somewhere else.

***

***

# ✅ RESPUESTAS (para ti)

## 🔹 Sección 1

1. ❌ has → ✅ **have finished**
2. ❌ have → ✅ **has seen**
3. ❌ has started → ✅ **had started**
4. ❌ went → ✅ **had gone**
5. ❌ have → ✅ **has just eaten**
6. ❌ has left → ✅ **had left**
7. ❌ had completed → ✅ **completed** (no necesita past perfect; “last week”)
8. ❌ have → ✅ **has become**
9. ❌ saw → ✅ **had seen**
10. ❌ went → ✅ **has gone**

***

## 🔹 Sección 2

11. ❌ had have → ✅ **had had**
12. ❌ had had had → ✅ **had had** (uno extra incorrecto)
13. ❌ has had had → ✅ **has had** (no necesitas doble “had”)

***

## 🔹 Sección 3

14. ❌ has closed → ✅ **had closed**
15. ❌ have made → ✅ **had made**
16. ❌ had have → ✅ **had had**

***

# 🎯 Cómo usar esto (clave)

Haz que el estudiante explique **por qué**:

Ejemplo:

> “Uso *had started* porque hay dos acciones en el pasado y una ocurrió antes”

Y para “had had”:

> “Es ‘había tenido’, entonces necesito had + had”

***

# 🔥 Tip avanzado (muy ICFES)

Dile que busque siempre:

1. **Dos eventos en pasado → past perfect**
2. **Palabras como “already/just” → present perfect**
3. **Errores típicos:**
   * has/have mal usados
   * participios incorrectos (gone, seen, made)
   * “had have” ❌
